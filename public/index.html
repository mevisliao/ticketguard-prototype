Marketplace

Status guide
Owned — Ticket is held by the current owner and is not listed for resale.
Listed — Ticket passed verification and is available for purchase.
PaymentConfirmed — Buyer payment has been confirmed.
TransferPending — Payment is complete and official ownership transfer is pending.
TransferCompleted — Transfer event recorded; after completion, the ticket returns to Owned for the new buyer.

Instructions
Seller steps
1. Enter the Ticket ID and seller account.
2. Check the displayed maximum resale price.
3. Enter a selling price within the limit.
4. Click Verify and list.

Buyer steps
1. Find a ticket with status Listed in Marketplace.
2. Filter by maximum price or Seat section if needed.
3. Select the ticket and click Buy this ticket.
4. Enter your buyer account and choose a payment method.
5. Click Pay and transfer.
